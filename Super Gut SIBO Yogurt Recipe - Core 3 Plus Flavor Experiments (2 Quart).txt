Super Gut SIBO Yogurt Recipe - Core 3 Plus Flavor Strains (2 Quart)

Overview
Core Super Gut probiotics plus Streptococcus thermophilus and Lactobacillus helveticus.

Core probiotics + half-and-half
- 2 capsules MyReuteri (L. reuteri)
- 2 capsules Biothin Probiotic (L. gasseri)
- Bacillus subtilis powder (approximately 1/6 tsp)
- 4 tablespoons organic inulin powder
- about 1.5 quarts organic half-and-half

Flavor strains (both in every batch)
- Tablet potency note: 3B CFU per serving of 2 tablets (so 1 tablet = 1.5B CFU)
- 9 tablets Streptococcus thermophilus (~18B CFU)
- 9 tablets Lactobacillus helveticus (~18B CFU)

Step-by-Step
1. Open/add the core probiotic capsules/powder into a bowl.
2. Add the 9 S. thermophilus tablets and 9 L. helveticus tablets.
3. Add 4 tbsp inulin and 4 tbsp half-and-half; whisk into a smooth slurry.
4. Add remaining half-and-half and whisk well.
5. Incubate at 98F for 36 hours.
6. If the yogurt separated during ferment, stir gently while still warm before refrigerating (easier to recombine than after chilling).
7. Refrigerate immediately after ferment (or after the optional stir in step 6).

Notes
- A 2-quart jar should work with about 1.5 quarts of half-and-half, leaving some headspace for foam/expansion.
- If lactic acid/sourness is too high, try 30–32 hour ferment in a future batch (change time only; keep dose and temp the same until you learn what that batch does).
