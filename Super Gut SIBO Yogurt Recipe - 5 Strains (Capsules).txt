Super Gut SIBO Yogurt Recipe - 5 Strains (Using Capsules)

This is a simplified version of Dr. William Davis's SIBO yogurt from his book Super Gut, combining the core 3 probiotics (Lactobacillus reuteri, Lactobacillus gasseri, Bacillus subtilis) with 2 additional strains for traditional yogurt taste while minimizing sourness. It yields about 8 servings (1 quart batch) and ferments for exactly 36 hours at 98°F to maximize bacterial counts (CFUs). 
The long ferment reduces lactose to near-zero, making it tolerable for many with dairy sensitivities. 
Start with 1/2 cup daily, building to 1 cup as tolerated.

Ingredients

Non-Probiotics:
•	2 tablespoons organic inulin powder (prebiotic to feed the bacteria)
    Source: NOW Foods Supplements, Inulin Prebiotic Pure Powder, Certified Organic, Non-GMO Project Verified (https://www.amazon.com/dp/B000MGSI1K)
•	1 quart organic half-and-half (ultra-pasteurized preferred for creaminess and reliability; avoid low-fat)
    Source: Central Market Organics Half & Half (https://www.heb.com/product-detail/central-market-organics-half-half/1771947)

Core Super Gut Probiotics [3]:
•	[O] Lactobacillus reuteri (DSM 17938, ATCC PTA 6475) (MyReuteri by Oxiceutics) (10B CFU; provides Lactobacillus reuteri) 
    Temperature: [96-100°F (36-38°C) / 98.6-109.4°F (37-43°C)] 
    Source: MyReuteri by Oxiceutics (https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0FBQQW4M1)
    Lactic Acid Production: Medium
•	[O] Lactobacillus gasseri (BNR17) (10–20 billion CFUs) 
    Temperature: [98-100°F (37-38°C)] 
    Source: Biothin Probiotic by Dr. Mercola (https://www.amazon.com/dp/B09CSFTM24)
    Lactic Acid Production: High
•	[Y] Bacillus subtilis (HU58) (10–20 billion CFUs; use approximately 1/12 to 1/6 tsp for 10-20B CFUs) 
    Temperature: [90-95°F (32-35°C) / 95-113°F (35-45°C)] 
    Source: Foods For Gut Bacillus Subtilis Probiotic Powder (https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B5M8WTFZ)
    Lactic Acid Production: None (Bacillus species)

Traditional Yogurt Taste [2] (use 1 or both):
•	[Y] Streptococcus thermophilus (St-21) (10–20 billion CFUs; creates thick, creamy yogurt texture, traditional yogurt culture) 
    Temperature: [107.6-113°F (42-45°C) / 68-122°F (20-50°C)]
    Source: Individual Strain Capsule (https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DSC3136P)
    Pill Amount: 3-7 tablets (each tablet contains 3 billion CFU; use 3-4 tablets for 10B CFU, 6-7 tablets for 20B CFU)
    Lactic Acid Production: Medium
•	[Y] Lactobacillus helveticus (10–20 billion CFUs; protein digestion, may help with blood pressure, supports gut health, produces bioactive peptides, traditional yogurt culture) 
    Temperature: [104-113°F (40-45°C) / 59-111.2°F (15-44°C)]
    Source: Individual Strain Capsule (https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DSC4J7G7)
    Pill Amount: 3-7 tablets (each tablet contains 3 billion CFU; use 3-4 tablets for 10B CFU, 6-7 tablets for 20B CFU)
    Lactic Acid Production: Medium

Starter Follow up Batches:
•	2 tablespoons previous SIBO yogurt (as starter) or
•	1/4 to 1/2 cup or all whey from previous strained batch
•	1–2 tablespoons inulin powder  
•	1 quart organic half-and-half
•	Note: Whey is less concentrated than yogurt, so use 2-4x more volume. Whey starter can be used for multiple batches - monitor quality and refresh with fresh probiotics when fermentation weakens. Whey mixes more evenly than yogurt starter.

Equipment
•	Mixing bowl and whisk
•	Yogurt incubator
•	Glass jars for storage (1 quart capacity)

Step-by-Step Instructions (Using Capsules)
1.	Open all probiotic capsules/tablets and empty contents into a mixing bowl:
    - 1 MyReuteri capsule (L. reuteri - 10B CFU)
    - 1 Biothin Probiotic capsule (L. gasseri - 10-20B CFU)
    - 3-7 tablets from Streptococcus thermophilus product (https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DSC3136P)
      * Each tablet contains 3 billion CFU
      * Use 3-4 tablets for ~10B CFU, or 6-7 tablets for ~20B CFU
    - 3-7 tablets from Lactobacillus helveticus product (https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DSC4J7G7)
      * Each tablet contains 3 billion CFU
      * Use 3-4 tablets for ~10B CFU, or 6-7 tablets for ~20B CFU
    - Add Bacillus subtilis powder (approximately 1/12 to 1/6 tsp for 10-20B CFUs)
2.	Add 2 tbsp inulin and 2 tbsp half-and-half to the powders; whisk into a smooth slurry.
3.	Pour in the remaining half-and-half and whisk thoroughly to combine.
4.	Pour into clean jars, cover loosely (e.g., with plastic wrap), and place in the incubator at exactly 98°F.
5.	Ferment undisturbed for 36 hours (start in the morning or evening for convenience; don't shorten, as strains peak after 24 hours).
6.	Refrigerate immediately after fermenting. It will thicken further in the fridge and store for up to 2 weeks. 

For Greek-Style Yogurt (Optional):
7.	Line a fine-mesh strainer or colander with cheesecloth, coffee filter, or nut milk bag.
8.	Place strainer over a bowl and pour the fermented yogurt into it.
9.	Refrigerate and let strain for 2-6 hours (longer = thicker yogurt).
10.	Save the whey for smoothies, baking, or as a probiotic drink.
11.	Transfer strained yogurt to a clean container and refrigerate for up to 2 weeks.

Tips
•	Texture/Taste: Expect thick, tangy yogurt with traditional yogurt flavor from S. thermophilus and L. helveticus. Separation is normal—stir or strain as needed.
•	Sourness: This combination includes 1 high lactic acid producer (L. gasseri) and 3 medium producers (L. reuteri, S. thermophilus, L. helveticus). This should produce moderate sourness (less than using L. bulgaricus). If still too sour, consider:
    - Reducing fermentation time to 30-32 hours
    - Straining the yogurt to remove whey (reduces lactic acid concentration)
•	Safety: Safe for pregnancy/breastfeeding (multi-strain dilutes Lactobacillus reuteri's oxytocin effect). If histamine-sensitive, start tiny (1 tsp) and monitor.
•	Reculturing: Use previous batch as starter for 4–6 cycles until it thins or sours oddly, then restart with fresh probiotics.
•	Sourcing:

    - Bacillus Subtilis from Probiotic Powder by Foods for Gut (https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0B5M8WTFZ) - 1/12 to 1/6 tsp per batch
    - Streptococcus thermophilus from Individual Strain Capsule (https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DSC3136P) - 3-7 tablets per batch (3 billion CFU per tablet)
    - Lactobacillus helveticus from Individual Strain Capsule (https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0DSC4J7G7) - 3-7 tablets per batch (3 billion CFU per tablet)

Lactic Acid Production Summary:
•	High Producers (1): L. gasseri
•	Medium Producers (3): L. reuteri, S. thermophilus, L. helveticus
•	Non-Producer (1): B. subtilis
•	Expected Sourness: Moderate (better than L. bulgaricus - should have traditional yogurt taste with less sourness)

KEY
[O] means probiotic will grow optimally at 98F.
[Y] means probiotic will survive and maybe even grow at 98F, but not optimally.
[N] means probiotic will die at 98F.
